Privacy Protocol

1. GEOSPATIAL DATA (GPS)

Tactical Range Card PRO utilizes high-precision GNSS sensors to calculate Earth-rotation (Coriolis) and target vector solutions. This data is processed locally on your device. We do not transmit your live location to external servers unless you explicitly engage the 'Tac Comms' mission mode, which shares location only with your authenticated team members.

2. OPTICAL & ACOUSTIC SENSORS

Access to your device's Camera and Microphone is required for situational awareness features (Local HUD View) and PTT voice communications. All video and audio data is processed end-to-end between your tactical units. We do not record or store your surveillance feeds on our infrastructure.

3. LOCAL STORAGE & INDEXEDDB

To ensure total offline reliability (Airplane Mode), all range cards, profiles, and intel snapshots are stored in an encrypted local database (IndexedDB) on your device. This data remains on your hardware until you manually delete it or clear the application cache.

4. THIRD-PARTY DATA SHARING

We absolutely do not sell, rent, or share your personal data, location data, or sensor feeds with any third-party advertising networks, analytics providers, or external organizations. Your tactical data belongs to you.

CONVERSIONS

MIL to MOA1 MIL = 3.438 MOA
MOA to MIL1 MOA = 0.29 MIL
Yards to Meters1 Yd = 0.9144 M
Meters to Yards1 M = 1.0936 Yd

QUICK FORMULAS

  • Angle Cosine (Slant)
    True Distance = Line of Sight × Cosine of Angle
  • True MOA (TMOA) vs SMOA
    1 TMOA = 1.047" @ 100y
    1 SMOA = 1.000" @ 100y
  • Wind Value Clock
    Full Value (90°) = 3, 9 o'clock
    Half Value (45°) = 1, 2, 4, 5, 7, 8, 10, 11 o'clock
    No Value (0°) = 12, 6 o'clock
