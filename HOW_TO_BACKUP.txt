HOW TO BACK UP THE TRC APP TO A USB DRIVE
=========================================

If you ever need to back up your files and I am not around, you can do it yourself in 5 seconds!

1. Plug your USB Drive into your computer.
2. Open your Windows File Explorer and see what letter your USB drive is (Usually D: or E:).
3. Right-click on the "backup_to_usb.ps1" file in this folder and select "Run with PowerShell".
4. A blue window will pop up asking for your USB drive letter. 
5. Just type the letter (like D) and press Enter.

The script will automatically grab EVERY SINGLE VERSION from your "All_Sandbox_Snapped_of_Current_Versions" archive and copy them perfectly to your USB drive!

Once it says "BACKUP COMPLETE", you can pull your USB drive out and you are good to go!
